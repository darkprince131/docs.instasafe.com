# Feedback or Suggestion

We try to constantly improve all our products. Any feedback or suggestions that you might have will help us a lot in developing the next release. If you have any suggestions, bugs report or issue, you can open a ticket by sending an email to [support@instasafe.com](mailto:support@instasafe.com).

If something is missing in the documentation, or if you found some part confusing, feel free to contact us with your suggestions for improvement. We are always open to your feedback at [support@instasafe.com](mailto:support@instasafe.com). We love hearing from you!
